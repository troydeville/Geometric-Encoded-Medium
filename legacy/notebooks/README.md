# Geometric Encoded Medium (GEM) – Discovery Notebook

This folder contains experimental and developmental code notebooks used during the construction of the **Geometric Encoded Medium (GEM)** framework. These notebooks document the trial-and-error process, computational validations, and progressive refinement of algebraic expressions central to the GEM model.

---

## 📂 Files

- **`GEM_Discovery_Notebook_0.1.0.pdf`**  
  A Mathematica-driven notebook exported to PDF. Contains hundreds of calculations, unit tests, dimensional checks, and exploratory expressions.

## 🧠 Contents Overview

The notebook includes:

- Early derivations of particle mass-radius relationships
- Tests of the vacuum compression ratio and Planck-scale scaling
- Gravitational derivations using GEM curvature formulations
- Unit conversions and expressions of constants
- Rydberg series reconstructions
- Algebraic expression building around:
  - \( \Gamma = \alpha m_P l_P \)
  - \( G \), \( \mu_0 \), \( \epsilon_0 \)
- Binding energy levels across fermions
- Surface gravity calculations for Earth, Sun, and neutron stars
